The initial twelve-section design request remains available in Git history:
https://github.com/pintoolx/market-making/blob/9801f752872909dee3da08c2e2fc2848a0bbbd4a/docs/strategy-builder-research/original-spec.txt

It is historical input, not the current product contract. Later decisions require
English product copy, a fixed verified Guard Extruction, immutable Provider
publication and Maker instantiation, and consented event-driven reevaluation.
Standing reports update only when effective terms change; they do not use
periodic ten-minute renewal. The original general ban on arbitrary execution,
custom contracts, unreviewed asset signing and backend custody still applies.

The current requirements retain the original core boundaries: deployment and
capability evidence, iterative design with revision invalidation, independent
draft/validated/compiled/simulation/transaction-plan models, exact arithmetic,
deterministic recipes and decoding, twelve bounded application tools, distinct
preview/quote/settlement evidence, wallet-reviewed ship/dock, shared-inventory
accounting, separate routing status, isolated testing and reproducible delivery.
