## Design Context

### Users
Strategy Providers design market-making strategies; Makers select strategies and supply liquidity under their own risk limits. Providers need visible templates, structured editing, version publication and contextual identity management.

### Brand Personality
Clear, approachable and deliberate. PinTool MM is a product, with concise English interface copy and meaningful transaction feedback.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the existing light blue surfaces, blue actions, outlined controls, PinTool identity and contour background. Retain the established typography and mobile layout. This task reorganizes navigation and restores authoring choices rather than changing the visual identity.

### Design Principles
- Keep the top-level navigation centered on Strategy and Liquidity.
- Put names and permissions beside the account or strategy they affect.
- Separate browsing and drafting from publication and onchain execution.
- Keep all six template choices visible without implying unsupported execution.
- Keep private policies out of public drafts and browser persistence.
